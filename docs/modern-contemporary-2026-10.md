# Modern and contemporary violin works, October 2026

The catalogue grew from **4,061** to **4,881** works: **820 works** by 182 twentieth-century and living composers were added (73 of them new to the catalogue, among them Augusta Read Thomas, Birtwistle, Tippett, Boulez, Xenakis, Stockhausen, Lachenmann, Dusapin, Escaich, Connesson, Eötvös, Larcher, Pintscher, Tabakova, Terry Riley, Michael Torke, Ludovico Einaudi, John Luther Adams, Sierra, Benzecry), and 139 cells in existing rows were corrected (106 durations, 22 titles, 5 accompaniments, 4 levels, 1 set, 1 category).

## Highlights
- **Philip Glass** 5 → 12 works (Strung Out, Pendulum, New Chaconne, Double Concerto, Sarabande, Epilogue, Candyman Suite added; Violin Concerto No. 3, "France" and Mad Rush arrangements were looked for and not found as published violin works).
- **Arvo Pärt** 5 → 10 (each published scoring of Fratres, Passacaglia and Tabula rasa, La Sindone, Estonian Lullaby).
- **Augusta Read Thomas** 3 → 19 (Concerto No. 1 "Spirit Musings", Concerto No. 3 "Juggler in Paradise", Astral Canticle, Rumi Settings, the solo caprices and enchantments). Gaia's Prayer, Aurora, Magnetic Flux, Helios Choros and Ritual Incantations are not violin works; Dancing Helix Rituals is a trio.
- Substantial gaps filled for Schnittke, Auerbach, Bacewicz, Rihm, Henze, Kancheli, Tubin, Milhaud, Martinů, David Matthews, Beamish, Hosokawa, Chen Yi, Fujikura, Sciarrino, Scelsi, Xenakis, Cage, Feldman and others; the interwar masters (Berg's Chamber Concerto, Hindemith's Kammermusik No. 4, Honegger, Antheil, Casella, Malipiero …) were audited.

## How it was done
- 15 research slices (`data/expansion/modern/groups.json`, ~220 composers) were researched by subagents against publisher catalogues, composer sites, IMSLP, library catalogues and Wikipedia work lists (brief: `data/expansion/modern/AGENT_BRIEF.md`, one output file per slice in `data/expansion/modern/web/`). Scope: violin as solo/principal instrument with a published score or a commercial recording; chamber music for three or more different instruments and works without a violin soloist are out (listed in each slice's `skipped`).
- Levels: researcher estimate + two blind graders (Opus and Sonnet, `GRADER_BRIEF.md`, `grades/`), final level = median of the three. They agreed closely (mean differences below 0.2 levels; 13 of 829 works spread by 3 or more levels). The same graders gave mode / pace / character tags (written to the Character column; "unsure" modes are left blank).
- Popularity comes from the researchers' `well_known` flag. No API spend: everything ran through Claude Code subagents.
- Decisions made by hand live in `data/expansion/modern/make_decisions.py` (corrections to existing rows) and `decisions_extra.json` (48 researcher entries dropped as unconfirmed or weakly sourced). `data/expansion/modern/regen.sh` rebuilds everything from the pre-modern snapshot (`c3dad39`); `scripts/expand/modern_merge.ts` is the merge.

## Verification
Every addition was re-checked by a second, independent agent (`data/expansion/modern/verify/`):
- The 260 additions that were low-confidence or whose scoring names another instrument or ensemble besides the violin got a full check (four agents): 215 confirmed, 39 fixed, 6 rejected.
- The remaining 569 got a light check (six agents; the given source plus a second look at scoring and violin role): 546 confirmed, 22 fixed, 1 rejected; 2 more were dropped by hand (Torke *Last*, no score or recording found; Glass *Epilogue*, the closing solo of a quartet score).
- Fixes were mostly durations; the substantive ones: Kilar *Oda Béla Bartók in memoriam* (violin with brass, percussion, harps, piano), Kernis and Escaich double-concerto titles, Revueltas *Toccata* (small ensemble), Penderecki *Duo concertante* (violin and double bass), Zwilich *Romance* (chamber orchestra), Bacewicz Sonata of 1929 (violin and piano; now "Sonata (1929) for Violin and Piano" so it is not confused with the solo sonata), Turnage *Shadow Walker* and Higdon *Concerto 4-3* (two violins).
- Rejected and removed: Matthews' arrangement of the Brahms Double Concerto, Sallinen *Chamber Music X*, Milhaud Impromptu Op. 91, Tabakova *The High Line*, Ponce *Canción de otoño*, Weir *For Friendship*, Tabakova *Such Different Paths* (a string septet).
- About 15 confirmed items still rest on a Wikipedia list plus one thin source.

## Recordings
`scripts/collect_recordings.py` (yt-dlp, metadata only) collected up to 8 YouTube candidates per new work; six reviewers picked the performance (rules as in `scripts/judge-recordings.ts`: the exact work, played on the violin, complete, right scoring, preferably professional), no API calls (`judge-recordings.ts export` / `apply`). 513 of the 748 works with usable candidates got an embeddable pick.

**Relaxed second pass.** The owner decided that unless clean uploads are plentiful, a video that features other pieces as well is better than nothing. For every piece still without a recording (353 new works and about 860 older pieces judged "none" or removed by the duplicate-video check) a second, plainer search ("<composer> <title>", cache `data/cache/yt2`) widened the candidate pool and ten reviewers re-judged under `data/expansion/modern/recordings/RELAXED_BRIEF.md`: an album, recital or compilation that clearly includes the complete work is accepted (`compilation: true` in `data/recordings-judged.json`, and exempt from the shared-video rule in `build-data.ts`); a single movement or excerpt of a multi-movement work, another work, or an arrangement for another instrument is still not. Run it with `RELAXED=1 CHUNKS=10 npx tsx scripts/judge-recordings.ts export` and `RELAXED=1 ... apply`. Result: 264 judgements applied, 218 pieces gained a recording (3,022 of 4,881 pieces now have one; 499 of the 820 new works), 66 of the picks are compilations. Picks marked `low` (unsure whether the work is in the video) are not shown. The 894 technical-materials rows have never been searched.

## Caveats
- 86 additions rest on one weak source (mostly a Wikipedia work list) and are marked `low` in `final/added.json`; about 400 are `medium` (one authoritative source). Publisher sites of Boosey & Hawkes, Wise, Schott and Chester are script-rendered and could not be read, so durations and years are blank for many works (about 100 additions have no duration).
- Existing rows the researchers doubted but that were left as they are (see `final/existing-issues.json`): Kirchner "For Solo Violin" (1455), Barber "Suite from Souvenirs" (704) and the F-minor Sonata (1604), Silvestrov 1267–1269 and 1519 (scoring), Ives "Largo" (1006), Dohnányi Op. 32c (902), Bacewicz Sonata numbering (1371), Szymanowski spellings (2812, 2813), Presto's "Op. 44" for Ben-Haim's sonata (1416).
- Not covered or only thinly: Tower, Frank, Shaw (sites unreachable), Babbitt and Wuorinen (Peters), Zhou Long, Yun, Takemitsu, Miyoshi, Ung, Hisaishi, Brouwer, Gismonti (no violin works found), Lutyens (manuscripts only), McDowall.
