Chamber music: the new pieces of the page

Static mockups for phase 1 of docs/chamber-music-plan.md, drawn with the site's own stylesheet and the 135 benchmark works (counts are real). Each part shows the desktop layout and a phone. Parts 1 and 2 are one proposal each; parts 3 and 4 have options to choose between.

1The instrument switcher

“Chamber music” sits under an “Ensembles” divider, below the instruments, so the list can grow (orchestral excerpts, duos) without mixing the two kinds. Until launch the violin pages keep today’s menu; /chamber already shows the right-hand version, so it can be tested.

2The finder sentence

Same three menus as the violin finder, with chamber words: the form (full works, short pieces, sets), the ensemble, and the level. The ensemble menu groups the eleven formations under small headings. The level bars and the “around / exact” switch are unchanged.

3A results row

Each row has to say who plays (the scoring, with published alternatives and ad lib. parts) and which part sets the level. Three ways to fit that into the current row. Same eight works in each.

4The “includes instrument” filter

“Show me what I can play”: works with a part for a given instrument, counting published alternatives and ad lib. parts (the Kegelstatt Trio counts for violin as well as clarinet; Eine kleine Nachtmusik for double bass). Shown here with clarinet chosen. Picking two instruments (options 1 and 2) means works that have both.
