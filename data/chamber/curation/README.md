# Chamber curation: plan (DRAFT for the owner's review)

Phases 2 and 3 of `docs/chamber-music-plan.md`: research subagents turn the IMSLP census (`data/chamber/census/CENSUS.md`) into catalogue entries, one composer slice each, and add the twentieth-century and living composers IMSLP lacks. **No curation agent has run yet**, and nothing here writes to `data/chamber/works.json`.

## Files
- `AGENT_BRIEF.md`: the brief every curation agent follows (scope, floor, one entry per work, benchmarks and cross-listing, the fields, house title style, nicknames and `aka`, alternatives and ad lib. parts, levels, sources, output format, working rules).
- `groups.json`: the phase-2 slices, generated by `npx tsx scripts/chamber/curation_slices.ts` from the census. A slice lists its composers (a big composer split by formation family: strings / piano / mixed), its IMSLP works, how many of them IMSLP links to a recording, and the works of the open-question groups (voice, viols).
- `groups-modern.json`: the phase-3 slices, written by hand: 287 composers in 17 slices by region and school. These composers are left out of the phase-2 slices; their IMSLP candidates (most have none) are in their phase-3 input. `modern-coverage.json` shows each one's IMSLP count.
- `input/<slice>.json` (not in git): what one agent gets; write them with `npx tsx scripts/chamber/curation_slices.ts --inputs`.
- `out/<slice>.json`: the agents' results (none yet).

## The slices (counts of 2026-10-16; `groups.json` has the current ones)

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `c` core | 51 | 246 | 5,662 | 2,512 | every candidate curated; gap check against a standard work list |
| `m` mid | 10 | 282 | 1,949 | 446 | every candidate curated |
| `t` tail | 24 | 3,831 | 13,061 | 27 | triage: one recording check per composer, then only what might pass |
| `x` modern | 17 | 287 | (their few IMSLP works) | | publisher catalogues and work lists; gap check |

Core is a composer with five or more works that IMSLP links to a commercial recording, a benchmark composer, or one of about 60 standard chamber composers that IMSLP's links under-count (`CORE_EXTRA` in the script: Elgar, Sibelius, Arriaga, Weber, Krommer, Danzi …). Sizes: about 130 works for a core slice, 250 for a mid slice, 600 for a tail slice (the vocal and viol works that wait for a decision are not counted). That is **102 agents** at about 90 tool calls each, all Claude Code subagents (no API-key spend).

## Proposed order
1. **Owner decisions first** (`CENSUS.md`, "Open questions"): vocal works with strings, viol consorts, recent uploads, the glass harmonica, names without a comma. The brief's defaults hold until then.
2. **Pilot of three slices**: `c20_haydn-string-quartet` (six benchmarks to match against), one mid slice (`m07_early-romantic-mayseder-veit`) and one tail slice (`t07_classical-c-f`). Check the output against the brief, the budget, the floor decisions and the house style; resize the slices if the agents run out of calls (tail slices are the risk).
3. Core slices, then mid, then tail, about eight at a time; then the 17 modern slices, which can run alongside since no composer is in two slices.
4. A merge script (not written yet) collects `out/*.json`, drops duplicates against `works.json` and between slices, and hands the new works to phase 4 (grading) and 5 (verification) as `docs/chamber-music-plan.md` describes.
