## Holds and useful negative cases

- Arie van Hoek's *Three Short Blueses* has [composer score evidence](https://arievanhoek.nl/onewebmedia/Music/AvHBlueAlt.pdf) of viola I–VIII and an [IMSLP page](https://imslp.org/wiki/3_korte_bluesjes_%28Hoek%2C_Arie_van%29) with premiere metadata. Its uploaded audio is explicitly synthetic. An independent concert programme would strengthen the floor; verify the exact version and composer's track record/exclusion status before proposing it.
- Another [van Hoek viola octet](https://imslp.org/wiki/Viola_Octet_%28Hoek%2C_Arie_van%29) has four synthesized audio files and no uploaded human recordings. A category and those MP3s alone cannot qualify it.
- Frank Proto's *Quartet for Basses* has composer/publisher score and programme-note leads, but an adjacent purchase catalogue route contained unrelated gambling content during the pilot. Hold the score link for a reliable alternate source; do not follow a corrupted storefront through unrelated links.
- Vivaldi RV 580 names four violins but requires accompaniment. It does not belong in the unaccompanied violin-only bucket.
- The existing eight-cello Pärt entries are an **audit queue**, not automatic benchmarks. Re-establish exact distinct parts and authorized versions, then verify recordings; ensemble headcount or an arrangement for string orchestra cannot establish an eight-part cello version.

## What the pilots changed in the plan

1. A version-specific performance programme is enough to establish actual use even without YouTube; do not make scarce repertoire disappear because of recording availability.
2. Split source work, musical version, edition and performance identity. An editor, a second engraving and a new arranger require different handling.
3. Grade and enrich the arrangement rather than copying the source work's facts. Preserve dates/conflicts as unresolved when sources disagree.
4. Keep scoring proof separate from performers' names; eight named players still need evidence of eight independent parts.
5. An initial end-to-end pilot must include a shared IMSLP collection, an existing id, a commercial arrangement with no public PDF, an exact-version performance with no approved YouTube, a named excerpt and a ten/twelve-part candidate. The last two cases still need to be selected during that pilot.
